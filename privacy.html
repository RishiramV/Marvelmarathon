MARVEL MARATHON ARCHIVE · PRIVACY

Your library. Your squad. Your choices.

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Marvel Marathon Archive is a fan-made watch companion operated by Rishiram V. Questions or account deletion requests can be sent to rishiramv2112@gmail.com.

Account and watch history

You can browse as a guest. Guest title and episode progress stays in your browser. Google sign-in provides basic account information, including your name, email and account identifier, to Supabase Authentication. The app uses your Google name on your dashboard and Flex cards. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or your Google password.

Cloud progress contains your account identifier, title identifiers, watched statuses, individual episode statuses and update times. Database rules restrict direct access to the owner. An accepted friend can view your watch history and calculated XP through the app. Other users cannot read your library.

Usernames, friends and chat

Creating a unique username is optional. If you enable username discovery, a signed-in user who enters your exact username can see your display name and username and send a friend request. Your email is not included in search or friend profiles. Discovery is off until you choose to enable it.

Accepting a request shares your watch progress and XP with that friend and enables direct chat. Messages are stored in Supabase and are available only to the participants while the friendship is accepted. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted. The operator and infrastructure providers may have administrative access needed to operate the service. Avoid sharing sensitive information.

You can decline requests, remove friends or block users. Removing or blocking stops access to shared progress and conversation history through the app; it does not erase the stored messages. Unblocking allows future requests and does not automatically restore a friendship. Messages and account information remain until removed by the operator or service lifecycle. Account deletion also removes the account's related progress, profile, connections and messages.

Shared groups

Groups are optional. A group owner can invite accepted friends, and each person chooses whether to join. Joining shares your display name and username with active group members and lets them read and send group messages. Group membership does not grant access to your watch history or XP; those remain available only to accepted friends.

Active members can read the group conversation, including earlier messages. Pending invitees cannot read messages or the member list. The owner can rename the group, invite or remove members, and close it to new messages. Leaving or removal stops your access to the conversation. Closing a group keeps its history readable to active members. Stored messages are not erased by leaving, removal or closure. Group chat is stored in Supabase and is not end-to-end encrypted. Account deletion removes the account’s messages and memberships; groups owned by the deleted account and their related messages are also removed.

Flex and XP

Flex cards are created in your browser and include your display name, username if configured, and selected watch progress. Downloading a card does not publish it. Sending a Flex cover to a friend or group stores a snapshot of your display name, username, selected collection or title, watch and episode totals, watch time, XP, edition and snapshot date in that conversation. The participants can view and download the cover; the snapshot does not update when your watch history changes. The Share button opens your device's sharing options where available. Anyone you share the image with can keep or redistribute it. XP is calculated from self-reported watch progress and is not a verified achievement or a redeemable currency.

Storage and providers

Supabase stores accounts, cloud progress, usernames, friendships, groups, group memberships and messages. Netlify hosts the site. Your browser stores your sign-in session, per-account cached progress, pending offline edits and old guest history retained for migration. Signing out does not erase local progress caches or guest backups.

The site loads the Supabase client from jsDelivr. Artwork may load from external image hosts, including TMDB and Wikipedia; episode information comes from TVmaze. These services receive ordinary web requests, which may include IP address, browser information and referrer. Hosting and authentication providers may retain operational and security logs under their policies.

Your controls

You can edit title and episode statuses, download a progress backup from the account menu, export a marathon schedule as PDF, change your username and discovery choice, and remove or block connections. Reset watch history changes watched statuses; it does not delete the authentication account. To request deletion, email the operator from the address linked to your account.

You can remove browser data in your browser's site settings. Sync pending changes first if you want to keep them. Google access can be revoked through your Google Account settings.
